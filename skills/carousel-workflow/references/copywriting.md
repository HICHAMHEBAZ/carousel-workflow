# Copy rules

## Cover hook
- 6-10 words (25.6% doubled, against 21.0% at 26+ words and 20.2% at 41+). Count the headline only; kicker and numeral are extra, but keep the cover uncluttered.
- Put a number in the hook for lists and how-tos (24.6% against 21.4%). Years and large figures work well. Not needed for stories or opinions.
- "Free" in the hook tested high (40% against 25.8%) but the sample is small. Use it only when it is true.
- Question hooks, ALL CAPS, "you/your" and text density showed no measurable effect. Do not force them.

## Every slide
- One idea per slide, at most 45 words of body text.
- Headline: 2 lines, one accent word. Short sentences, active verbs.
- Headlines are questions or tensions, not labels.
- Numbers as digits. Keep source figures exact; round only if the source rounds.
- Last slide: a single save/send CTA. No links on slides.

## Language notes
- **Arabic (MSA)**: set direction to RTL; the numeral sits at the inline-start (left) side, text and series mark on the right. Give an established Arabic term first and the original in context if the concept is obscure. Western digits are fine and read faster.
- **Darija tone**: only when the user asked. Keep headlines MSA and flavour the caption and the comment question.
- **LTR languages**: mirror the layout; the numeral sits at the right.
- Avoid machine-translated phrasing. Read each line aloud once; if you would not say it, rewrite it.
- Optional pass: run a humanizer skill on the caption and the long-form slide text, in a separate step from writing.

## Credits
Always credit the author and outlet on the last slide. The top-right mark is the user's series name, never another outlet's logo.
